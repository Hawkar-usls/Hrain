Findspot/date: Tell Ibn es-Salam (Mendes), February 1902  
Published catalogue dimensions: length 12.2 cm; mass 7 g; gold sheet about 0.1 mm

## Provenance binding

The object is part of the gold mummy-trapping assemblage from the 1902 Mendes burial associated with Nesbanebdjedet. The Metropolitan Museum of Art independently directs readers to Cairo CG 53465–53469 for the gold-leaf trappings of this burial. Vernier's catalogue and Plate LXXXVII resolve the phallic envelope specifically as **CG 53469 / JE 35425**.
